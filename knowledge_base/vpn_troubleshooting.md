# VPN Troubleshooting Guide

## Symptoms

Employees may report:

- VPN will not connect
- VPN disconnects
- Corporate resources are unavailable remotely

## Approved troubleshooting steps

1. Confirm the employee has a working internet connection.
2. Restart the VPN client.
3. Confirm the employee is using the approved corporate VPN application.
4. Restart the laptop.
5. Try connecting to the VPN again.

## Security

Employees must not disable security software to make the VPN work.

Employees must not install unapproved VPN applications.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The VPN service appears unavailable for multiple users
- Authentication repeatedly fails
- The employee receives a security or certificate warning
