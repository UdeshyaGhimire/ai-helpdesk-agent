# Password Reset Guide

## Symptoms

Employees may report:

- Forgotten password
- Account locked
- Cannot sign in after changing password

## Approved troubleshooting steps

1. Ask the employee to use the approved password reset portal.
2. Confirm the employee is using their corporate username.
3. Ask the employee to create a new password that meets company requirements.
4. Ask the employee to sign in again using the new password.

## Security

Never ask an employee to provide their password.

Never send passwords through email or chat.

## Escalation

Escalate to the identity and access team when:

- Self-service password reset does not work
- The account remains locked
- There are signs of suspicious account activity
