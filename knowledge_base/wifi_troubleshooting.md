# Wi-Fi Troubleshooting Guide

## Symptoms

Employees may report:

- Laptop cannot connect to Wi-Fi
- Wi-Fi disconnects repeatedly
- Connected to Wi-Fi but internet does not work

## Approved troubleshooting steps

1. Confirm Wi-Fi is enabled on the laptop.
2. Confirm airplane mode is disabled.
3. Disconnect and reconnect to the corporate Wi-Fi network.
4. Restart the laptop.
5. Check whether another device can connect to the same Wi-Fi network.
6. If multiple employees cannot connect, escalate the issue to the network team.

## Escalation

Escalate to the IT network team when:

- Multiple employees are affected
- The corporate wireless network is unavailable
- The employee cannot reconnect after completing the approved steps
